# 👨‍🍳 Keep Cooking
## keep on cooking keep on living

Keep Cooking is the next step toward utterly obliterating any friendship you have with your friends. Trapped in a chaotic VR kitchen by mischievous aliens, your mission is to cook the perfect dish. The catch? You have no idea how to prepare the meal! To succeed, you’ll rely entirely on your friend playing on the PC, who must guide you by dictating the right ingredients and steps.

But it won’t be easy... prepare to face rebellious eggs, levitating toast, and a whirlwind of culinary chaos as you work together (or not) to create a flawless five-star meal. Can you handle the heat, or will your friendship go up in flames?
