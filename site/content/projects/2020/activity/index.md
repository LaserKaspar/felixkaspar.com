Game nights are one of my favorite ways to spend time with friends and family, but there was always one small problem: we’d run out of words!

So, I decided to solve that problem by building a simple Progressive Web App (PWA). This app randomly generates words that can be used for playing activity. It’s quick, lightweight, and works offline, so you don’t need to rely on an internet connection (or someone’s dwindling creativity).

If you’ve ever found yourself stuck without ideas mid-game or even pre-game, this app might just be what you need.
